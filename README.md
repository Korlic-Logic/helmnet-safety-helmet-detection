# HelmNet Safety Helmet Detection

Computer vision project classifying whether workers in industrial/construction images are wearing safety helmets.

## Business Context

Workplace safety in hazardous environments like construction sites and industrial plants is crucial to preventing accidents and injuries. One of the most important safety measures is ensuring workers wear safety helmets, which protect against head injuries from falling objects and machinery. Non-compliance increases the risk of serious injury, and manual oversight is prone to errors — especially at scale. HelmNet aims to automate this monitoring using image classification.

## Objective

Build and evaluate deep learning (CNN) models that classify whether a worker in an image is wearing a safety helmet, to support automated compliance monitoring.
